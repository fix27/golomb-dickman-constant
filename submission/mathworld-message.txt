Subject: Update for "Golomb-Dickman Constant Digits": 40,000 certified digits

Dear Dr. Weisstein,

The MathWorld page "Golomb-Dickman Constant Digits"

  https://mathworld.wolfram.com/Golomb-DickmanConstantDigits.html

states that 15,000 digits have been computed. I would like to report
40,000 digits, certified by interval arithmetic rather than computed in
floating point.

The certificate gives integers Lambda_- < Lambda_+ and a scale
S = 133074 with

  floor(10^40000 Lambda_- / 2^S) = floor(10^40000 Lambda_+ / 2^S),

so the interval [Lambda_-/2^S, Lambda_+/2^S] containing lambda does not
cross a decimal boundary and the truncation is proved. Its width is
about 10^-40042.26, i.e. 42 guard digits beyond the 40,000 published
ones. The computation uses the trapezoidal rule after the Takahashi-Mori
substitution, explicit tail and discretisation bounds, Stieltjes
continued fractions, and Arb balls.

Certificate, digits, code and a dependency-free verifier:

  https://github.com/fix27/golomb-dickman-constant/tree/main/40000

The package also extends the OEIS b-file for A084945 from 101 to 40,000
terms, and extends A225242: the blocks 55555 and 666666 do not occur in
the 40,000 digits, so a(5), a(6) > 40000.

All statements on the page that can be checked against digits were
reproduced: the digit tables for 10, 100, 1,000 and 10,000 digits, the
first-occurrence positions, the Earls sequence values 28, 256, 1967, 387,
the six prime truncations at 6, 27, 57, 60, 1659 and 2508 digits, and the
absence of 0123456789 and 9876543210. No discrepancy was found.

For verification I have prepared a small archive that separates three
levels: a dependency-free check of the certificate and the digits (seconds),
an audit of the bounds with a sample of nodes recomputed from the formulas,
and an independent recomputation of the leading digits with a different
quadrature rule and a different library. The archive is

  golomb_dickman_40000_verification.zip

in the repository above. The independent recomputation is numerical rather
than rigorous, and the rigorous enclosure still needs an Arb re-audit, which
I could not run on my machine because of a local security policy.

If it is appropriate, could you tell me what level of independent
confirmation would be enough for the "not independently confirmed" note to
be revised? I would rather aim at the right target than at a larger number
of digits.

I would be glad to provide the certificate in another format, or the
first N digits for any N <= 40,000.
